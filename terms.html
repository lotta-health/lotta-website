2. What Lotta is, and what it isn't

Short version

Lotta keeps your own record of life on a GLP-1 medicine, shows it back to you, and uses it to pick what you read and the questions you may want to bring to your clinician. It isn't a medical device, and it doesn't give medical advice.

What Lotta does

  • Records what you tell it: your medication and doses, your symptoms and how you're feeling, and, if you switch them on, your meals and your weight. Also anything you write in your own words.
  • Shows that information back to you, in your own words and numbers.
  • Selects which of our articles and content you see, based on what you've recorded.
  • Turns what you've logged into questions you may want to bring to your clinician, including a PDF you can choose to share with them.

That's the whole list. Everything in Lotta is either something you put there, or something we wrote before it could appear.

What Lotta doesn't do

  • It doesn't diagnose anything, or tell you what's wrong.
  • It doesn't tell you what dose to take, when to take it, or whether to change or stop.
  • It doesn't tell you whether a symptom is serious, normal, or nothing to worry about.
  • It doesn't predict anything about your body, and it doesn't work out how much medicine is in it.
  • It doesn't replace your doctor, nurse or pharmacist, and it isn't a second opinion.

Decisions about your treatment are between you and the people treating you. Lotta's job is to help you arrive at those conversations with your own information in front of you.

If it's an emergency

Don't use Lotta. Call your local emergency number, or go to your nearest emergency department. Nobody at Lotta watches what you log, and writing something in the app doesn't reach anyone who can help you.
