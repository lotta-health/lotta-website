Questions and answers

Lotta is a long-term companion app designed to help people navigate every stage of their GLP-1 journey, combining evidence-based guidance, practical tools and compassionate support to create lasting lifestyle change.

Lotta covers all approved brands available on the market, based on your location, as well as all available treatment types, including custom dosing.

No. Lotta is built with the entire journey for GLP-1 takers in mind, from the moment you start considering it to after you've entered maintenance.

No. Lotta is not a medical device and it does not diagnose, predict, advise, identify symptoms, provide any treatment plans or suggestions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to your nearest emergency department.

During the beta we will give testers free access to the app and collect your feedback on the features, how it looks, what you'd like to see and what feels helpful to you. Participating users will receive a link via Apple's TestFlight and will be able to download the beta app and sign up.

You can apply for the beta here. Please note that the beta will only be available for iOS.

We don't sell it, we don't use it for targeted advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models. You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it or delete it by reaching out to privacy@lotta.health. You can find further information on our Privacy Policy.
