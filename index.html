Progress isn't linear. Support shouldn't be either.

Things your treatment doesn't have to be.

  • another crash diet
  • ignoring side effects
  • perfect every day
  • chasing a number
  • putting your life on hold
  • something you figure out alone
  • all or nothing

Your treatment should fit your life, not the other way around.

Alongside your care, never instead of it.

Lotta does not replace healthcare professionals — it helps you stay informed between appointments.

  • No shame, no pressure, no countdowns.
  • We help you note things and prepare questions — decisions stay with you and your clinician.

It's ok to…

  • ask your care team questions
  • have a bad day
  • need support
  • eat something you didn't plan
  • change your plan with your clinician
  • have a week where progress feels invisible
  • not have everything figured out
