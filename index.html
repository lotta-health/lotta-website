Our promise

Alongside your care, never instead of it.

Lotta does not replace healthcare professionals — it helps you stay informed between appointments.

  • No shame, no pressure, no countdowns.
  • We help you note things and prepare questions — decisions stay with you and your clinician.
  • Your health data is handled with care, built in Europe.
